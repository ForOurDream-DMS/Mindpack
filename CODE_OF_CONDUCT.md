# Code of Conduct

Mindpack follows a simple contributor standard: be respectful, stay focused on the project, and do not expose private data.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction quickly.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused and reviewable.
- Use synthetic examples instead of real private conversations or customer data.
- Respect copyright, licenses, and source ownership.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Posting another person's private data, credentials, or private conversations.
- Submitting copyrighted or paid content without rights.
- Repeatedly derailing issues or pull requests.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates these expectations.
